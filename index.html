Why we exist

Every business — small or large — deserves its own website, its own product, its own AI.

That is the whole idea behind Rythmn AI. The corner store, the clinic, the studio, the factory, the scale‑up — each one should have a digital home it actually owns, a product that carries its business online, and AI working quietly inside it. Not a rented page on someone else's platform. Not a template with their logo dropped in.

So we build the same way for a first‑time owner as we do for a funded team: real engineering, real ownership, and a path that keeps going after launch.

Where we are today

  • 14 service lines — websites, web apps, SaaS, e‑commerce and mobile, designed and built in‑house.
  • AI where the work happens — assistants, RAG systems and agents grounded in your own content.
  • Cloud, DevOps and integrations — AWS and GCP, shipped with CI, monitoring and an SLA.
  • Marketing OS — our live AI marketing product: a week of Facebook and Instagram posts, briefed and scheduled.
  • Astro — our live Vedic astrology app: kundali, matching, numerology and AI answers from your chart.

How we work

Four bars, then you're live

Every engagement runs the same four bars. You see working software at the end of each one — not a status deck.

BAR 01 · WEEK 1

Discover

We map the goal, the users, the systems and the constraints, then agree on what "done" means before anyone opens a file.

BAR 02 · WEEK 2

Design

Flows, screens and architecture. You approve a clickable prototype and a written technical plan, not a wireframe you have to imagine.

BAR 03 · WEEKS 3–10

Build

Weekly cuts on a live staging URL, behind CI and tests. You review real software every Friday and steer the next week.

BAR 04 · ONGOING

Run & grow

Launch, monitoring and an SLA — then we keep iterating on whatever the numbers and your users say next.
